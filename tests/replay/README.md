# tests/replay

Headless checks, plain Node, no browser and no dependencies. CI runs all three.

- `run.mjs` (the engine's referee; it belongs to the engine area): simulates 1000 full games and fails on any out without a legal reason, any half inning without exactly 3 outs (walk-offs excepted), a broken batting order, a score that differs from the runs recorded, a game that does not terminate or end legally, a seed that does not replay identically, a fielder who covers ground or throws faster than a person can, or a fielder waiting to tag in the lane of the runner sliding into his base. It re-derives each out from the fielder actions in the event stream instead of trusting the engine, checks that `engine/` stays pure (no DOM, three.js, clock, unseeded randomness, network or viewer imports), and checks that the engine's count tables have one probability for every ball-strike count.
  - Sample: seeds `SEED` to `SEED + GAMES - 1` (default 1 to 1000). Game n uses the n-th of the 12 home/away pairings of the four teams in turn, so every team plays every other team at home and away and the rates belong to the league, not to one matchup. The run prints the stream digest and the league-wide rates.
  - Rate check: with 1000 games or more, the run also fails when a league-wide rate (runs, K%, BB%, HBP%, AVG, OBP, SLG, BABIP, HR, 2B, 3B, GIDP, IBB, SB, CS, pitches per PA, zone, swing, zone swing, chase, whiff) leaves its tolerance around the reference league (MLB 2025, `REFERENCE` in the file). A tolerance is about three standard errors of a 1000-game sample plus a little room for the model; tolerances are only ever tightened.
  - With fewer games, for example `GAMES=200 SEED=5000 node tests/replay/run.mjs`, the rates are printed but not checked; every other check still runs.
- `commentary.mjs`: the fallback bank is well formed, drafts are deterministic, a line never depends on later events, commentary never changes the game, registered scripts match their game. It also runs `commentary/tension.check.mjs` (tension index: range, determinism, causality, bases vs engine frames, ordering of known situations) as a child process, so CI covers it.
- `static.mjs`: every file the page and its modules refer to exists, every script parses, the workflow folder and the rule files keep their limits. It also runs `tests/names.check.mjs` (league name rules: no real-athlete full name from `league/tools/blocklist.txt`, no repeated full name, a given name at most twice in the league and never twice on a team) and `tests/staff.check.mjs` (`league/staff.js`: four roles per team, free numbers, lines of at most 30 characters, umpire crews deterministic and legal, no clock or unseeded randomness, never imported by `engine/`), `tests/ratings.check.mjs` (`league/ratings.js`: every overall and ability a whole number 1 to 100, league mean in range, never imported by `engine/`), `tests/opening.check.mjs` (the frozen first game `league/preseason/opening.json`: own hash, the pin `FROZEN`, same engine version re-simulates to the same game against the frozen rosters `league/preseason/rosters-opening.js`, codec round trip) and `tests/site.check.mjs` (production host shows no development tools: `ui/site.js` host rules, every dev control inside `.dev-only`) as child processes.
