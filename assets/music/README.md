`pregame.mp3` is 成為妳的月, the pregame music bed (#83).

The `track-*.mp3` files are the owner's song pool (handed over 2026-11-22). **No use is assigned yet and nothing
loads them**: a window does not pick one by itself; the owner says which track goes where, and that is written on
the issue that needs it. The number in the file name is the owner's own track number (two of his files carry the
number 4, hence `04a` and `04b`; he sent no 3, 9, 16 or 22). "Cover", "Remix" and "Remastered" are versions he
made of his own songs, not other people's songs. All are MP3 160 kbps stereo.
